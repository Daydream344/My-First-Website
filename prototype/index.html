Overview

The official Finder Library consists of 11 graphic novels. They are all different stories, focusing on different characters, all building up into one beautiful, complex world. The first one was published in 1999, and the last as recent as 2021. They were written and hand drawn by author, Carla Speed Mcneil. She describes it as an "aboriginal science fiction". It takes story lines from recognizable aboriginal and modern urban societies set on a far off future Earth.

Main Characters

Jaeger

A wandering "finder" with a complicated past and a knack for showing up just when you need him

Emma Lockhart

A member of the Llaverac clan. She is the embodiment of a person living in a dream. A survivor of abuse, her storyline is an interesting one.

Brigham Grosvenor

A member of the medwar clan. An abusive husband and father who fed fear into his wife and children to make them cling to him.

Rachel Grosvenor

Child of Emma and brigham Grosvenor. A young woman navigating trauma, identity, and the strange social structures of Anvard.
